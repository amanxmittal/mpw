About Our Enterprise

Decades of Trusted Manufacturing in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh

Established in Meerut — India's premier sports goods manufacturing cluster — Mittal Plastic Works has been a dependable injection moulding partner to leading enterprises, wholesalers, and brands across India.

Led by Mr. Vineet Kumar (Vineet Mittal), our enterprise specializes in precision-engineered plastic cores, protective jalli assemblies, and custom-moulded components. We combine high-grade polymers with precision tooling to create components that withstand demanding industrial use.

We take immense pride in our long-standing relationships with clients across India, supported by prompt communication, transparent pricing, and punctual delivery timelines.
