Our Heritage

From Meerut's Industrial Cluster to Pan-India Supply

Mittal Plastic Works was established in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh — the sports goods capital of India. Under the hands-on leadership of Mr. Vineet Kumar (Vineet Mittal), the company focused on solving critical mechanical and packaging challenges for sports goods manufacturers and industrial converters.

Over the past two decades, we have continuously reinvested in our manufacturing facility in Sabun Godam, Baghpat Road. Today, we operate high-capacity injection moulding machinery capable of producing millions of plastic cores, jalli grids, and custom components each year.

Our philosophy is grounded in engineering honesty: selecting the correct polymer grade for each application, maintaining zero-defect tolerances, and never compromising on shipment schedules.

Our Mission

The Four Pillars of Our Manufacturing

Every batch leaving our facility is backed by decades of technical refinement.

Virgin Polymer Selection

We work exclusively with certified raw materials (Virgin PP, HDPE, Nylon) ensuring optimal elasticity, tensile strength, and impact resistance.

Precision Tooling & Dies

Our moulding dies are engineered to micro-millimeter tolerances, eliminating flash, sink marks, or parting-line inconsistencies.

Multi-Shift Quality Audit

Continuous dimensional checks, weight uniformity measurements, and drop impact tests ensure zero-defect packaging for every shipment.

Dependable Logistics

Rapid dispatch schedules from Meerut connecting straight into Delhi-NCR, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP, and national transport networks.
