How We Develop Custom Moulded Components

A streamlined four-step engineering process from initial CAD or physical sample to high-volume multi-shift manufacturing.

01

Design Review

You provide a physical part or 2D/3D drawing. We analyze wall thickness, draft angles, polymer selection, and shrinkage parameters.

02

Tooling & Die

We coordinate hardened steel die fabrication, runner gates, and cooling channels optimized for high-volume cycles.

03

Trial Sampling

Initial samples are pressed and dispatched to your facility for fitment, stress testing, and dimension sign-off before full production.

04

Bulk Production

Continuous scheduled machine runs, automated batch packing, quality auditing, and scheduled pan-India freight dispatches.
